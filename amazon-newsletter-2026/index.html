The placement

What ran, and where

The feature sat in the body of the edition, labelled "In partnership with Amazon.co.za", with one clear button to start a seller account.

  • Edition: "Side Hustles to Start Before the Year Ends", a theme about earning extra income, which matches selling online.
  • Sent: Tuesday 27 October 2026 at 09:03, South African time.
  • Audience: the full SME South Africa list of 52 957 subscribers: South African small business owners, founders and people starting out.
  • Message: why sell on Amazon.co.za, Somizi's Sompire brand as proof, three steps to start, the R1 a month seller offer and the Black Friday opportunity.
  • Call to action: one button, "Explore selling on Sellercentral.Amazon.co.za", linking to sellercentral.amazon.co.za with Amazon's own tracking code.
