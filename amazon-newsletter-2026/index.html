The placement

What ran, and where

The feature sat in the body of the edition, after the opening stories and the event invitation, under the label "In partnership with Amazon.co.za".

  • Edition: "Side Hustles to Start Before the Year Ends", a theme about earning extra income, which matches selling online.
  • Sent: Tuesday 27 October 2026 at 09:03, South African time.
  • Audience: the full SME South Africa list of 52 957 subscribers, South African small business owners, founders and people starting out.
  • Message: why sell on Amazon.co.za, Somizi's Sompire brand as proof, three steps to start, the R1 a month seller offer and the Black Friday opportunity.
  • Call to action: one button, "Explore selling on Sellercentral.Amazon.co.za", linking to sellercentral.amazon.co.za with Amazon's own tracking code.

Building on it

Where the next placement can go further

A dedicated email

A send about selling on Amazon.co.za alone, so the offer is the subject line and the whole email, not one feature among several.

The readers most likely to sell

Target the members already reading about e-commerce, retail and side hustles, who are closest to opening a seller account.

Black Friday timing

A reminder close to Black Friday, when the R1 a month offer and the festive season give sellers the strongest reason to start now.
