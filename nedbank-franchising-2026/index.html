Part two

The feature article on smesouthafrica.co.za

"The Economics of Eating Out: The Evolving Model of QSR" was published on Monday 12 October 2026. It looks at how digital ordering, delivery and drive-thru are changing the restaurant and quick-service business, and what that means for franchisors and franchisees. Nedbank's franchising expertise runs through the piece, and four links lead to its retail franchising page.

The article stays on the site, so it keeps working long after the email: readers can find it through Google and through the site's articles at any time.
