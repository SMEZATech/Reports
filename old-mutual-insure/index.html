Summary

How the campaign performed

The Old Mutual Insure campaign for Multisure business insurance went live on SME South Africa's Facebook, LinkedIn and Instagram pages on 23 October 2026. This report covers reach, engagement and clicks for each channel, with a link to every post. The last section covers the Multisure article on smesouthafrica.co.za for October 2026.

What the numbers show

  • Facebook delivered most of the reach. It accounted for 82% of impressions and 85% of clicks (231 of 272), and reached 12,893 people.
  • LinkedIn had the higher engagement rate of the two channels that carried a link. At 2.30% it was 1.7 times Facebook's 1.33%, and 41 people clicked through to the quote page.
  • Instagram had the highest engagement rate of the three. It recorded 171 views and a 2.34% engagement rate from a small audience. Instagram feed posts cannot carry a link, so there were no clicks from this channel.
  • Every click went straight to Old Mutual's Multisure quote page. None went to an SME South Africa article, so each of the 272 clicks came from someone heading to a quote.

What the numbers show

  • The people who found it read it. 63% of visits were engaged visits, more than twice the site average for October, and 3 readers scrolled to the end.
  • It sent readers to the quote page. 1 in 7 readers clicked through to Old Mutual's Multisure page from inside the article.
  • Readers came back to it. 5 returning readers accounted for 13 of the 22 views.
  • A business audience, reading at work. 21 of 22 views were on a computer, and most readers in South Africa were in Gauteng (Benoni, Randburg and Johannesburg).
  • Google is starting to show it. The article appeared 58 times in Google results in its first month, for searches such as "business protection", "insurance for SMEs", "cover for business risks" and "insurance for start-up businesses in South Africa".
