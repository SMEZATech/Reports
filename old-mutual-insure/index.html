At a glance

Facebook brought the volume. LinkedIn brought the most engaged audience.

What the numbers say

  • Facebook carried the reach. It delivered 82% of all impressions and 85% of the clicks (231 of 272), reaching 12,893 people.
  • LinkedIn had the most engaged audience of the link channels. Its 2.30% engagement rate is 1.7 times Facebook's (1.33%), and 41 people clicked through to the quote page.
  • Instagram added awareness. It had 171 views and a 2.34% engagement rate, the highest of the three, on a small base. Instagram posts can't carry a link, so it didn't add clicks.
  • Every click went to Old Mutual's Multisure quote page, not to an SME South Africa article, so all 272 clicks were prospective quote requests.

Recommendations

How to turn this into more quote requests

1

Put budget behind LinkedIn

LinkedIn had the strongest engagement of the two link channels. A sponsored version of the "18%" post, targeted at owners and directors of small businesses in South Africa, is the most direct route to more quality clicks.

2

Keep Facebook as the volume channel

Facebook delivered 85% of the clicks. Follow up with the second post from the agreed copy pack ("Fire. Theft. Vehicles. Your people. Your stock."), which lists the covers in a checklist that's easy to read on a phone.

3

Give Instagram a link

Add an Instagram Story with a link sticker to the Multisure quote page, and the six-slide carousel from the copy pack, so Instagram can drive clicks as well as awareness.

Closing the loop on quote requests

The campaign's links were set up to carry UTM tags (utm_campaign=multisure, with the channel as the source). If Old Mutual shares the quote requests recorded against those tags, we can report the full path from impression to quote request and set targets for the next flight.

How to read this report

Definitions and sources

Impressions
The number of times the post was shown on screen (Facebook, LinkedIn).
Views
Instagram's count of how many times the post was seen.
Reach
The number of different people who saw the post (Facebook).
Engagements
Reactions, comments, shares, saves and clicks on the post.
Clicks
Link clicks to Old Mutual's Multisure quote page.
CTR (click-through rate)
Clicks divided by impressions.
Engagement rate
Engagements divided by impressions, or by views on Instagram.
Source
Each platform's own analytics for the SME South Africa page, as at 9 November 2026.
